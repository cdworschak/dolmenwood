# THE WEBS OF OLD AUNT SPINDEL — Pointcrawl (Hex 1211)

**Goal:** Rescue Farnham Ribblemead + the book, and if possible the captive cleric of St Signis.
**Antagonist:** Old Aunt Spindel — mad, theatrical, genuinely smart. She uses misdirection and hostages before she ever fights fair.
**Pacing:** designed to run in **one session, spilling into a second at most.** If your table is slow, cut node D or G entirely — the crawl still works with just A→B→C→E→F.

## HOW TO RUN IT (Travel Points)

- Moving along any link costs the **Turns** listed on that link.
- **Off the marked links:** +1 Turn and a **Save Versus Hold** per character or entangled.
- **Lost/Encounter check:** 1-in-6 per node arrival off the direct route. On a hit, 4-in-6 it's **1d3 giant spinning spiders**.
- **Webs (entangle):** can't move or act; Strength Check each round (forgoing other actions) to escape; 3-in-6 chance 1d3 giant spinning spiders arrive in 1d3 rounds.
- **Burning webs:** 1 round to torch; everyone still inside takes 1d6 fire damage.

## THE NODES

Each node below is split into **"Obvious at first glance"** (read/describe immediately) and **"Revealed when searched"** (found only if the party actually looks).

### [A] Entry — The Sagging Boundary
**Obvious at first glance:** Grey strands appear between the trunks, hung with dried leaves and small dead birds. A dead stag hangs cocooned 20′ up, long drained.
**Revealed when searched:** Nothing — this node is deliberately calm, the last quiet moment before the webs start in earnest.

- Link to **[B]**: 1 Turn, no roll.

---

### [B] Webbed Thicket — the hub
**Obvious at first glance:** Rope-thick strands crisscross at every height; visibility drops to 20′. Faint, overlapping voices drift from the direction of **C** — laughter, weeping, a badly-acted argument, layered over each other, never quite human.
**Revealed when searched:** Drag-marks and torn Bogwitt Manor livery lead toward **C** (an obvious lure). A subtler sign — claw-marks climbing bark up and away from the thicket — marks the start of the canopy route to **G**, easy to miss unless someone thinks to look up.

- Careless movement here: Save Versus Hold or entangled.

**Real choice:** toward **C** (loud, informative, walks into Spindel's favourite stage) vs. toward **D** (quiet, but you can smell it before you see it) vs. up via **G** (most physically dangerous, gives zero warning, best shot at surprise).

Links: **[B]→[C]** 1 Turn, entangle save · **[B]→[D]** 1 Turn, entangle save · **[B]→[G]** requires finding the sign; 1 Turn to climb.

---

### [C] Marionette Glade — the show
**Obvious at first glance:** A web-swathed clearing reeking of carrion. Twelve corpse-marionettes act out a play — some declaiming lines in cracked, borrowed voices, others "seated" as a mocking audience.
**Revealed when searched/observed a round or two:** the "play" is a garbled reenactment of Farnham's capture — Spindel is workshopping it. Confirms Farnham is alive and specifically interesting to her, not just food.

- **Old Aunt Spindel is hidden** in a tree above (voice-throw range 60′), directing the show and staying hidden unless the marionettes are clearly losing.
- **Interrupting the show:** the 12 marionettes attack — see the **Manikin** stat block below.
- **Smart-play option:** playing along, flattering the "playwright," addressing her directly can draw her out of vanity — a rare early parley chance, at the cost of her now being fully alert.
- Loud fights here: +1-in-6 to all further encounter checks this crawl (she knows they're coming).

### [D] Charnel Bower — the nursery
**Obvious at first glance:** The smell hits first — a hollow beneath a fallen, cocooned tree, floor thick with husks, bones, and dozens of pulsing egg-sacs.
**Revealed when searched:** buried in the husks, a half-dissolved holy symbol of St Signis — confirmation the cleric came this way, and didn't come out clean.

- **No roll needed:** disturbing the bower (hard not to) hatches **1d6 giant spinning spiders** immediately.
- Fire works well here but everyone still inside takes the 1d6 fire damage too, spiders and PCs alike.
- Opens onto a back approach to **[F]** Spindel doesn't watch closely — reward for clearing it.

Links onward: **[D]→[E]** 1 Turn, entangle save · **[D]→[F]** 1 Turn, **no entangle save, no watcher** — but only if the spiders here were dealt with first.

---

### [E] Hanging Larder — the captives
**Obvious at first glance:** A dozen cocoons hang at various heights, most old and husked-out. Two are fresh.
**Revealed when searched/cut down:** one fresh cocoon holds **the cleric of St Signis** — alive, entangled, weak, already bitten once (half current HP), but stable. The other holds a half-mummified Bogwitt Manor servant, already dead.

- Freeing the cleric: cut them down (1 round), then a Strength Check to break the webbing, or a blade does it in one cut.
- **Real time pressure:** if C or D went loud, 2-in-6 per Turn spent here that Spindel sends spiders, or comes herself, to "collect new material."
- Once freed and able to speak, the cleric confirms: Farnham lives, still translating under duress in Spindel's tree-hollow; the book never leaves her sight.

---

### [G] Canopy Walk — the hard shortcut
**Obvious at first glance:** A rough, webbed highway through the branches, torn open by something huge moving through regularly.
**Revealed when searched:** nothing extra — this route is a speed/information trade, not a clue node.

- Each character: Save Versus Hold **and** a Strength/Dexterity check, or fall into the webs below near **D** (auto-entangled, normal spider-arrival chance).
- Skips C and D entirely — zero information, zero warning, but a real shot at surprising Spindel at **F** if the crossing goes clean.

Links onward: **[G]→[E]** 1 Turn if successful · **[G]→[F]** direct if the party chooses to skip E outright (make sure this is a knowing choice — no cleric rescue, no intel).

---

### [F] Spindel's Tree — the finale
**Obvious at first glance:** A vast, hollow-boled tree wound in silk, lit by guttering candle-stumps. Farnham Ribblemead — an old man, ink-stained, exhausted, ankle lightly chained more as theatre than real restraint — sits at a desk of woven web-cord, the book open before him. Old Aunt Spindel is here, mid-performance, unless the party arrives unnoticed.

**Farnham Ribblemead:** an old scholar, no stats needed — frail, talkative once safe, has no combat use but knows the book intimately and will cooperate fully the moment it's clear the party isn't another threat to perform for.

**Old Aunt Spindel (Large Monstrosity — Sentient — Chaotic)**
Level 8 AC 14 HP 45 Saves D8 R9 H10 B11 S12
Att Bite (+7, 1d8+2 + poison — Save Versus Doom or die in 1 Turn), Speed 20, Webs 40
Morale 9 XP 1,040

**Play her smart:**
- She does **not** open with a straight fight. Her first move is to grab or threaten Farnham (and the book) and to talk — she loves an audience and a negotiation almost as much as a performance.
- **If the party was noticed or fought loudly earlier (C, D, or a failed G crossing):** Spindel already knows Farnham matters to them — she's been told or has guessed as much from the noise, and she will lean on that immediately and hard: chaining him closer, putting her chelicerae-arms near his throat the instant things go wrong, and using every subsequent round of combat as another threat against him rather than a straight attack on the party. She won't kill him casually — he's valuable to her too — but she'll make every inch of this fight cost the party dearly for his safety.
- If forced to fight, she throws her voice (60′) to confuse her position, retreats into web she knows and the party doesn't, and directs any surviving marionettes or spiders as a distraction while striking from an unexpected angle.
- If losing badly, she doesn't fight to the death — Chaotic and self-interested, not suicidal. She may flee into deep web, or make a last desperate bargain (Farnham/the book for her life), or threaten to bite him rather than lose outright. Telegraph this as a threat first, not a surprise kill.
- **Sample lines** (adapt freely — she never talks in one voice for long):
  - *(sweet, motherly, aimed at Farnham)* "Hush now, dear, we're almost at the good part — don't spoil it for our guests."
  - *(mocking, thrown from nowhere)* "Oh, BRAVO. A rescue! I do love a rescue scene, the timing is never quite right, is it—"
  - *(if cornered, sudden and flat, dropping the act)* "You want the little man? You want the BOOK? Then you'll want to think very, very carefully about your next move."
  - *(if genuinely offered a way to break her curse)* "...say that again. Say it slowly. Say it like you mean it, and perhaps — perhaps we needn't do this the ugly way."
- **Burning her webs** works here too — a real tool by now, but risks the book on the desk. Their best weapon against her is also a threat to what they came for.

**If Spindel is killed:** any surviving marionettes anywhere in the hex immediately collapse, lifeless.

**Treasure (Spindel's hoard, stashed in a sack up in the tree):**
1,436cp · 802sp · 303gp · a fairy-silver necklace studded with jet (1,400gp) · a platinum ring bearing the Pollard family crest (800gp — ties to hex 1209) · a **Ring of Control (Fungi)**. The cleric's holy symbol (skull crowned with ivy) hangs among Spindel's trophies here too, easy to spot and return once the fight's over.

## GETTING OUT

Once Spindel is dead, fled, or bargained with and the immediate danger is over, there's no further real threat to overcome — just time. Retracing the crawl back out costs the same Turns as coming in, minus whichever links were cleared (D→F and the nursery, if handled, stay clear on the way back too).

**Stragglers:** any giant spinning spiders that weren't dealt with earlier (survivors from D's nursery, or ones that arrived mid-fight and got left behind) are still out there and don't know Spindel is gone. Treat leaving as a normal Lost/Encounter check per node passed through (1-in-6, 4-in-6 spiders) rather than a new danger — a mopping-up nuisance, not a second climax. If the party burned out D entirely or killed every spider they roused, the way back is clean.

## GIANT SPINNING SPIDER — full stat block

6′ long, black spiders with a dull but wicked cunning. Fill the wild places of Dolmenwood with their pernicious webs.

Medium Bug—Semi-Intelligent—Chaotic
Level 3 AC 13 HP 3d8 (13) Saves D11 R12 H13 B14 S15
Att Bite (+2, 1d6 + poison) or web (+2, entangle, range 20′)
Speed 20 Webs 40 Morale 8 XP 90 Enc 1d3

Poison: Save Versus Doom or die in 1 Turn.
Webs: A victim hit by a web attack must Save Versus Hold or become entangled in the sticky strands: –2 Attack and Save penalty, unable to move.
Breaking free: An entangled victim who forgoes other actions may make a Strength Check each Round to escape.
Burning webs: Webs can be destroyed by fire in 1 Round. All creatures in a flaming web suffer 1d6 damage.

## THE THREE REAL CHOICES (for your prep, not the players)

1. **B: loud (C) vs quiet-but-grim (D) vs risky-overhead (G).** Each trades information for danger differently.
2. **C: fight vs. play along.** A genuine chance to get Spindel to reveal herself early and possibly parley, at the cost of her being fully alert going forward.
3. **F: fight vs. bargain.** Her Desires (to feed, to perform, to become human again) are real hooks — a party that offers her something toward breaking her curse has a legitimate alternate ending available, not just a boss fight.
